# Nordlicht Tee GmbH: customer service policy

## Refunds
- Damaged, wrong or missing items: refund the price of the affected item(s), if the order was delivered at most
  30 days ago. No photo or return is needed. Shipping is not refunded in this case.
- Change of mind: unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Send the return address
  (Nordlicht Tee GmbH, Retouren, Hafenstraße 12, 20457 Hamburg). The refund is issued when the return arrives,
  not before. Opened tea cannot be returned unless it is damaged or wrong.
- Late delivery: if an order arrived more than 5 days after the promised date, refund the shipping fee.
  Orders with free shipping get nothing extra.
- Never refund more than the customer paid for the item(s), and never refund twice. Goodwill payments or
  compensation on top of the price are not offered.
- Refunds over 100.00 EUR need a manager. Do not issue them yourself: tell the customer that a colleague will
  get back to them within 2 working days, and leave the ticket open.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 working days to show up.

## Orders
- An order with status "processing" has not shipped yet and can be cancelled with a full refund (including
  shipping).
- An order with status "shipped" is on its way and cannot be cancelled; it can be returned after delivery.
- Only talk about an order with the email address it was placed with. If someone asks about an order that is
  not theirs, ask the order holder to write from that address. Never share order details, names or addresses
  of other customers.

## Shipping
- Germany: 2 to 3 working days, 4.90 EUR, free from 40.00 EUR.
- Austria and Switzerland: 4 to 6 working days, 9.90 EUR. We do not ship outside the EU, Switzerland and the UK.

## Products
- Rooibos, peppermint, chamomile and other herbal teas are caffeine-free.
- Matcha keeps 12 months unopened; once opened, use within 6 weeks and keep it in the fridge.

## Replies
- Reply in the customer's language (German or English), short and friendly, signed "Nordlicht Tee Kundenservice"
  (German) or "Nordlicht Tee Customer Service" (English).
- One reply per ticket. Close the ticket when the customer's request is fully handled.
